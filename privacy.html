Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Who we are

Hillman Group ("we", "us") is a real-estate developer based in Siliguri, West Bengal, India. Our projects include 360 East, One Landmark, One Landmark Commercial and Ekagra. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us – on WhatsApp, through our advertisements on Facebook and Instagram, by phone or at our sites – and how we use and protect it.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name and mobile / WhatsApp number, and your email address if you give it.
  • Your enquiry: the messages you send us, the project and type of home or commercial space you are interested in, your budget, timeline, purpose of purchase or lease, preferred site-visit dates and similar details you choose to share.
  • How you found us: if you message us after tapping one of our advertisements, Meta shares which advertisement you came from.
  • Our interactions: notes of calls and site visits, quotations and cost sheets we send you, and your preferences about receiving messages.
  • Channel partners: if you are a broker or channel partner who registers a buyer with us, the details you submit on our registration form.

We do not ask for bank, card, PAN or Aadhaar details on WhatsApp. These are collected only at the time of booking, through our office.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and share project details, brochures, floor plans, videos, prices and quotations.
  • To arrange and confirm site visits and to follow up on your interest.
  • To send you updates and offers about our projects on WhatsApp – you can stop these at any time (see section 7).
  • To keep a record of your enquiry so that our sales team can help you without asking the same questions again.
  • To show relevant advertisements and to avoid showing our ads to people who have already bought from us or asked us not to contact them (see section 5).
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory requirements, including those under RERA.

4. Automated replies

Some of our WhatsApp replies are prepared by an automated assistant that uses artificial intelligence to answer common questions about our projects quickly, at any time of day. Our sales team supervises these conversations and takes over whenever you ask to speak to a person or when a matter needs personal attention. You can ask to talk to our sales manager at any time.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run our business, and only as needed:

  • WhatsApp messaging providers – Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business) and our WhatsApp business-messaging service provider, to send and receive messages.
  • Cloud services – Google (Google Workspace) to store our enquiry records, and hosting and AI service providers that process messages to prepare replies on our behalf.
  • Advertising – Meta (Facebook / Instagram). We may upload phone numbers, first names and city of people who have enquired with us in a scrambled (hashed) form, so that Meta can show our ads to interested people or exclude existing customers. Meta matches this data and does not use it to identify you to us.
  • Banks and channel partners – only when you ask for a home loan or are introduced to us by a channel partner, and only the details needed for that purpose.
  • Authorities – where required by law.

6. How long we keep it and how we protect it

We keep enquiry records for as long as needed to help you and for our legitimate business and legal purposes, generally up to 3 years after our last contact, and longer for customers who book with us as required by law. Access to our records is limited to authorised staff, protected by passwords, and our service providers are required to keep the data secure.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Stop messages: reply STOP on WhatsApp, or tap the "Stop messages" button, and we will not send you further promotional messages.
  • Access, correct or delete: you can ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Withdraw consent: you can withdraw your consent to our use of your information at any time, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

To make any of these requests, contact us using the details below.
